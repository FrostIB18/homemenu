Home Menu – Family Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: Home Menu doesn't collect any data. Your meals, plans and shopping lists stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer can't see them.

What the app stores, and where

Home Menu stores the things you enter: meals and their ingredients, the week's plan, shopping list ticks and extras, pantry staples, and your family's meals of the day and their times.

  • On your iPhone. In the app's own storage. The Home Screen widget reads a copy of the next two weeks' plan from the same iPhone.
  • In your iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit so your data syncs. This is part of your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Family sharing

If you invite your family, the shared menu lives in the iCloud of the person who sent the invite. Apple shares it only with the people who accept the invite, and each of them can see and edit it. Whoever sent the invite can stop sharing at any time, and members can leave at any time.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or developer servers.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Information from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage figures, such as how many people installed the app. This is controlled by Apple and by your settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Delete meals, plans and list items in the app, and they're also deleted from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
