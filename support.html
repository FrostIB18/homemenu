Questions

How do I share the menu with my family?

On Today, tap Invite, then Invite family, and send the link, for example in Messages. Each person opens it on their own iPhone, signed in to iCloud with their own Apple Account. Everyone then sees and edits the same menu.

Changes don't appear on the other phone.

Check that both iPhones are online and signed in to iCloud, and that iCloud storage isn't full. Then pull down on Today, Week or Shopping to refresh. Edits made offline are sent once the phone is back online. If two people edit the same thing, the latest edit wins.

How do I leave a family, or stop sharing?

On Today, tap Family. Members tap Leave family to go back to their own menu. Whoever sent the invite taps Manage family to remove people or stop sharing.

Can I change the meals of the day?

Yes. Under Family → Meals of the day, add, rename or remove meals and set the time Today moves on to the next one. The whole family shares one setting.

Why isn't salt on my shopping list?

Pantry staples you always have at home stay off the list. They're listed under Pantry staples on the Shopping screen. Tap one to add it to this week's list. To change which ingredients are staples, edit a meal and tap Pantry next to the ingredient.

The Apple Watch app is empty.

The watch gets the menu from your iPhone. Open Home Menu on the iPhone the watch is paired with, and the menu appears on the watch shortly after.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.
