Week

The whole week at a glance.

Monday to Sunday on one screen. Fill the gaps from your own meals, plan leftovers or a night out, or skip a meal. Auto-fill and copying last week do the rest.

Shopping

One list, already added up.

Every ingredient for the week, totalled by name and unit, with extras like coffee or soap. Pantry staples such as salt stay off the list until you need them. Tick things off in the shop and everyone sees it.

Meals

Your family's own recipes.

Keep the meals you actually cook, with their ingredients and the times of day they suit. A few starter meals help you begin.

Apple Watch

The list on your wrist.

See the meal that's due now, the rest of today and tomorrow. In the shop, tick things off without taking out your phone. Your iPhone passes the ticks on, so the whole family sees what's been bought.

Family

Shared with your family. Private from everyone else.

Invite with a link and everyone sees and edits the same menu, even edits made offline. The menu lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There are no accounts to create, no servers in between and no data collected. Read the privacy policy
