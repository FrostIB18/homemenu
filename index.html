The family's menu for the week.

Plan breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner for every day, get one shopping list for the week, and share both with your family through iCloud. Everyone sees and edits the same menu.

  • Today

    The meal that matters now, switching at each meal's time. After dinner it shows tomorrow's breakfast.

  • Week

    Monday to Sunday at a glance. Fill the gaps from your own meals, plan leftovers or eating out, or skip a meal.

  • Shopping

    Ingredients for the week added up by name and unit, with your extras. Pantry staples stay off the list.

  • Meals

    Your family's recipes with ingredients and the times of day they suit. Starts with a few to try.

  • Family

    Invite with a link. The menu lives in your own iCloud; there are no accounts to create and no servers in between.

  • Your day, your way

    Set your family's own meals of the day and their times, and see what's next in the Home Screen widget.
